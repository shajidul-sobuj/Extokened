# 🧠 Extokened — Project Memory & Product Context

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Team:** Extokened
- **Positioning:** A builder-led technology team that learns, competes, collaborates, and ships practical products.
- **Visual Direction:** Dark editorial-tech / modern digital studio. Premium, precise, confident, **not an overused neon cyberpunk template**.
- **Audience:** Hackathon organizers, recruiters/mentors, potential collaborators, general visitors, existing members.

## 2. Palette & Design Tokens
- Background: `#080B12`
- Surface: `#101724`
- Elevated Surface: `#151F2E`
- Primary Text: `#F4F7FB`
- Secondary Text: `#A6B1C2`
- Muted Text: `#718096`
- Primary Accent: `#7C5CFF` (Primary CTA, links)
- Secondary Accent: `#22D3C5` (Highlights, status)
- Border: `#253044` (Dividers, card outlines)
- Success: `#34D399` (Verified wins)
- Warning: `#FBBF24` (Pending / in-progress)

## 3. Core Product Principles
- **Credibility over Hype:** Real work, evidence-backed achievements, substantiated numbers only.
- **Categorical Clarity:** Participation, finalist placement, and wins are tracked and displayed separately.
- **Content Discipline:** Dynamic detail pages only where justified by real content (`/projects/[slug]`, `/hackathons/[slug]`).
- **Accessible Motion:** Restrained, purposeful animations respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`. No blocking loaders, no scroll-jacking, no high-frequency strobe.
