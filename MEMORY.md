# 🧠 Extokened — Project Memory & Product Context

## 1. Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Team:** Extokened
- **Positioning:** A builder-led technology team that learns, competes, collaborates, and ships practical products.
- **Visual Direction:** Dark Editorial Engineering. Premium, confident, curious, technical, collaborative. Art-directed digital studio feel.
- **Explicit Exclusions:** No generic purple/blue AI gradient blobs, no floating glass cards, no glowing neon lines, no particle canvas loops, no decorative fake terminal windows, no repetitive 3-card sections.

## 2. Palette & Design Tokens (UI/UX Spec v0.1)
- `canvas`: `#0A0A0B` (Main dark solid background)
- `surface-1`: `#111214` (Section surfaces)
- `surface-2`: `#191B1F` (Cards / raised elements)
- `surface-3`: `#22252A` (Hover / active surfaces)
- `text-primary`: `#F3F1EC` (Main headings and copy)
- `text-secondary`: `#AAA9A3` (Body copy)
- `text-muted`: `#777873` (Technical metadata)
- `border-subtle`: `#292B2E` (Fine structural dividers)
- `border-strong`: `#414449` (Focused / emphasized boundaries)
- `accent`: `#C6F36A` (Proposed acid-lime accent)
- `accent-ink`: `#10120A` (Text on accent surfaces)
- `success`: `#74C69D` (Verified success state)
- `warning`: `#E8B86D` (Pending / podium state)

## 3. Typography & Grids
- **Display / Headings:** Space Grotesk
- **Body / UI:** Inter
- **Metadata:** JetBrains Mono
- **Grid:** 12-column desktop grid with 7/5 editorial splits, horizontal proof bands with vertical dividing rules, mixed project grids.

## 4. Core Product Principles
- **Credibility over Hype:** Real work, evidence-backed achievements, substantiated numbers only.
- **Categorical Clarity:** Participation, finalist placement, and podium wins are tracked and displayed separately.
- **Accessible Motion:** Restrained, purposeful animations respecting `prefers-reduced-motion`. No blocking loaders, no scroll-jacking, no high-frequency strobe.
